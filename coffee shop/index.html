Our Story

Rooted in craft,
steeped in warmth

Ember & Oak was born from a single obsession: the perfect cup. We source our beans directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — forging relationships that span seasons and continents.

Every roast is done in-house, every espresso pulled by hand. Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
