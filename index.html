What We Inspect

Thorough. Systematic.
Uncompromising.

Our expert inspectors go through every inch of your new home using professional-grade equipment and a structured checklist.

⚡

Electrical Systems

Complete check of wiring, switch boards, sockets, earthing, MCBs, and load testing of all circuits.

🚿

Plumbing & Fixtures

Water pressure, drainage, pipe leaks, fixture alignment, and hot/cold water supply verification.

🪟

Doors & Windows

Frame alignment, smooth operation, sealing, lock mechanisms, and hardware quality inspection.

🎨

Final Paint & Finish

Surface uniformity, colour consistency, wall seepage detection, and finishing quality assessment.

🏠

Flooring & Tiling

Hollow tile detection, grout quality, levelness, chip or crack checks, and finishing grade review.

🏆

Overall Quality Assurance

A comprehensive audit with a detailed digital report, photo documentation, and remediation guidance.
